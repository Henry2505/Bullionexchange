Frequently Asked Questions

Who is eligible for the Capital Support program?

The program is open to traders aged 18+ who have at least 6 months of live trading experience, demonstrate consistent profitability, and are ready to commit to full-time, on-site training at our Lagos headquarters. Applicants must pass our assessment process to qualify.

How will I be contacted after applying?

Our team will review your application within 3-5 business days. Successful candidates will be contacted via your submitted email, Telegram, or WhatsApp to schedule an interview. Please ensure your contact information is accurate and check your messages regularly.

What is the typical capital allocation for new traders?

Initial allocations range from $10,000 to $50,000 depending on your experience and assessment results. As you demonstrate consistent profitability and risk management, your capital allocation increases through our tiered system, with top performers managing over $500,000.

What are the program fees and profit splits?

There are no upfront fees to join the program. We operate on a performance-based model where traders receive 60-80% of their profits depending on their tier level. We cover all technology, data feeds, and mentorship costs.
