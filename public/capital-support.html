Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about our Capital Support program

What are the requirements to qualify for Capital Support?

We look for traders with at least 1 year of consistent trading experience, a verifiable track record, and a solid trading strategy. Applicants must demonstrate discipline, risk management skills, and a commitment to full-time trading.

How much capital can I access through this program?

Initial funding starts at $25,000. Based on performance, traders can scale up to $500,000. Our top performers have access to seven-figure accounts.

Is there any cost to join the program?

There's no upfront fee. We operate on a profit-sharing model where successful traders share a percentage of their profits with CBE. This aligns our interests with your success.

What kind of mentorship is provided?

You'll receive one-on-one coaching from senior traders, weekly strategy sessions, psychological training, and access to our proprietary market analysis. Mentorship is tailored to your specific needs and trading style.

Are there any geographical restrictions?

Our program is available globally, but requires physical presence at our Lagos office during market hours for collaboration and mentorship. We provide relocation assistance for qualified candidates.
